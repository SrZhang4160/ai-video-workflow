Interviewer: design a URL shortener.
Do you grab the pen and start drawing boxes?
Wait. Step one is asking about requirements.
Functional: what the system does.
Non-functional: how well it does it.
How fast, how reliable, how many users.
A URL shortener needs two things:
a long URL goes in, a short one comes out;
open the short link, land on the original.
Click stats too? Ask. Scope matters.
How fast? Say, redirect within a hundred milliseconds.
How reliable? It shouldn't just go down.
How big? How many users? More reads or writes?
Why ask first? The answers shape the diagram.
Far more clicks than creates? Add a cache.
Must stay up? Run several servers.
But every box you add has a cost.
What it can do: functional.
How fast, reliable or big: non-functional.
To sum up: ask what it does, then how well.
Once both are clear, start drawing.
