Your service is up ninety-nine point nine percent of the time. Sounds solid?

Availability is the share of time the service works.
The rest is your allowed downtime.

Take zero point one percent of that: eight point seven six hours.

Each extra nine cuts downtime by ten times.
Four nines: about fifty-three minutes. Five nines: about five minutes.
But every extra nine costs far more.
Common fixes: replicas, more data centers, auto failover.
Five minutes is too fast for a human fix.
