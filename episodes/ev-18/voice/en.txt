The two drift apart in three ways.
First, request types. Our cases are mostly broken headphones.
But real users ask for partial refunds, or exchanges.
We never tested those, so the score can't reflect them.
Then, phrasing. Our cases are written neatly,
with the order number, item and reason all there.
Real users write "that thing broke, where's my money?"
With no order number and some anger, it easily goes off track.
Finally, things change. Say the shop changes its policy,
some products now get fifteen days for refunds,
but the cases still say thirty days, so they're out of date.
