Five fundamentals. One: a clear task, exactly what you want done.
Two: context. Who is it for, and why do they need it?
Three: constraints. Length, tone, and what to avoid.
Four: the output format. Bullets, a table, or Jason.
Five: examples. Show what a good answer looks like.
