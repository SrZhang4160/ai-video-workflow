Want to learn Rust but not sure where to start? Let's look at the big picture first. It's really just five blocks, stacked one on another.
At the bottom: the basics. Variables, functions, conditions, loops — if you know Python, this part goes fast.
Layer two is ownership — the soul of Rust. Each value has one owner. To use it, borrow it. No garbage collector, and memory stays safe.
Layer three: modeling your data. Structs describe data, enums list the possibilities, and traits define shared behavior.
Layer four: real projects. Cargo runs the project, Result handles errors, and cargo test runs your tests.
At the top: advanced topics. Iterators, closures, smart pointers, concurrency and async.
If you can only go deep on one block, pick ownership. It is the hardest and the most important.
To sum up: move fast on the basics, and really master ownership.
