One memory read vs. one request across continents:
how many times slower is the second one?
First, three units: nanoseconds, microseconds and milliseconds.
Each step up is a thousand times longer.
One memory read: about 100 nanoseconds.
One SSD random read: about 100 microseconds.
About three orders of magnitude: 1,000 times slower.
A round trip inside one datacenter: about half a millisecond.
Across continents and back: on the order of 100 milliseconds.
That's the speed of light. More servers won't help.
If one memory read took one second,
an SSD read would take about seventeen minutes,
a datacenter trip over an hour, a cross-continent one 10+ days.
So: hot data in memory, servers close to users;
don't wait on cross-continent calls one by one in a loop.
The cost: memory is pricey, and multi-region is complex.
Memory in nanoseconds, SSD in microseconds, network in milliseconds,
Cross-continent: about a million times slower. Save this!
