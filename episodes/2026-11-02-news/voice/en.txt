One quarter. Nine hundred ninety-three million orders.
Third-party A I agents, in total so far?
Maybe several hundred orders.
Last Wednesday, on DoorDash's earnings call,
C E O Tony Xu said third-party agents
sent maybe several hundred orders in total.
Never fight gravity, he said, but don't be delusional.
Compare the total so far with one quarter's orders:
it's under one in a million.
Still, DoorDash already built the road:
an M C P connector lets agents find items and order,
once employees sign in and agree to the terms.
Amazon began blocking agents from buying.
Walmart opened an M C P gateway instead.
From November, customers can require per-order approval
or set a spending limit.
My take: the plumbing exists. Trust is missing.
If you build agents, design human approval
and spending limits first.
And watch: will agent orders become a reported metric?
In short: the road is built, the orders are not.
Trust wins, not the API.
