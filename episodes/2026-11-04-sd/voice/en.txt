The interviewer asks: one hundred million daily users. What is the Q P S?
Q P S means queries per second.
It's one formula. Done in thirty seconds.
First, requests per day: daily users times requests per user.
Then divide by the seconds in a day: eighty-six thousand four hundred.
Say one hundred million users, ten requests each: one billion a day.
Divide by eighty-six thousand four hundred: about twelve thousand Q P S.
It's an estimate, not an exact number.
For mental math, round eighty-six thousand four hundred up to one hundred thousand.
One billion divided by one hundred thousand is ten thousand. A bit low, but the right order of magnitude.
But traffic is not flat: quiet at night, busy in the evening.
A common rule of thumb: peak is about two to three times the average.
So the peak is about twenty-four to thirty-six thousand Q P S.
Size for the average, and the peak takes you down.
Size for the peak. The cost: idle servers off-peak.
To sum up: daily users times requests, divided by eighty-six thousand four hundred, is the average Q P S.
Multiply by two to three for the peak. Save this one!
