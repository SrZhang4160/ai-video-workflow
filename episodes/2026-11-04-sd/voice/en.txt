Estimating Q P S: three steps.
Step one: total requests.
People times requests per person.
Ten million times ten: a hundred million.
Step two: divide by the peak seconds.
Not the eighty-six thousand four hundred seconds in a day,
but the ten-minute peak: six hundred seconds.
A hundred million over six hundred: about one hundred seventy thousand Q P S.
Step three: count the servers.
Peak divided by one server's Q P S,
plus thirty to fifty percent headroom.
At two thousand each: eighty-five servers and up.
Remember: averages lie. Plan for the peak.
Quick quiz: a million people arrive in one minute,
six requests each. Roughly what is the peak Q P S?
